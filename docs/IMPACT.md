# Measurable Impact

> ⚠️ **The numbers below are illustrative assumptions, not measured results.** They show *how* we will measure impact. Replace them with real pilot data before quoting them anywhere.

## Illustrative response-time model

Generated by `sanjeevani compare --km N` (source: `src/sanjeevani/impact.py`).

**Assumptions**

| Parameter | Normal roads | Flood-affected roads |
|---|---|---|
| Road distance ÷ straight-line distance | 1.4 | 2.0 |
| Ambulance average speed | 30 km/h | 15 km/h |
| Time to mobilise ambulance | 10 min | 10 min |
| Drone speed / launch time | 70 km/h / 3 min | 70 km/h / 3 min |

**Result (minutes until first help arrives)**

| Straight-line distance | Road, normal | Road, flood | Drone | Time saved (normal) | Time saved (flood) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 km | 24.0 | 50.0 | 7.3 | 16.7 | 42.7 |
| 10 km | 38.0 | 90.0 | 11.6 | 26.4 | 78.4 |
| 20 km | 66.0 | 170.0 | 20.1 | 45.9 | 149.9 |

The drone delivers first-aid supplies and telemedicine support. It does not replace hospital care. The saving is time to *first help*, not time to full treatment.

## What a real pilot should measure

| Metric | How |
|---|---|
| Time from SOS to first help (drone vs road) | Timestamps in the system log |
| Triage agreement with a doctor | Compare system level to clinician's level on the same case |
| Dispatch success rate | Missions completed ÷ missions planned |
| Telemedicine connect time | SOS to doctor on call |
| Payload delivery accuracy | Distance of drop point from patient |
| Cases reached that roads could not serve | Count during floods |
